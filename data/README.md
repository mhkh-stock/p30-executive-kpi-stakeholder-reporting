# Data — NoorMart (SAMPLE)

Fictional retailer, Jan–Dec 2026. Generated by `src/generate_data.py`
(seed 30, deterministic) — re-running regenerates byte-identical
files. Nothing here is a real shop's books; the method is what is
real, and every KPI in the pack is recomputed from these files.

## Files

| File | Rows | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| `raw/daily_sales.csv` | 2,230 | One row per sale line: order, date, customer, product, qty, price, cost, returned flag |
| `raw/monthly_targets.csv` | 12 | The management plan: revenue / orders / margin % / returns % / new-customer targets, one row per month |
| `raw/monthly_prior_year.csv` | 12 | 2025 monthly totals (revenue, orders, new customers) so YoY is computed, not typed |
| `raw/data_manifest.json` | — | Counts + the plan (planned revenue × demand multiplier per month) the checks verify against |
| `processed/noormart.db` | — | SQLite star schema built by `src/load_data.py` — **not committed**, rebuilds from the scripts |

## The plan behind the numbers

Planned monthly revenue is the shop's plan (Ramadan/Eid bump in
March and May, wedding-season peak in November). Targets sit 3%
above the plan. Actuals come from the sale lines: each month is
closed when its net revenue reaches planned × a demand multiplier,
so the RAG mix is earned — 6 months beat target, 3 missed it by
more than 5%, 3 landed within 5%.

- **Net revenue = gross sales − returns.** Returned lines earn
  nothing: 84 lines came back in 2026, PKR 496,937 of PKR 13,983,480
  gross (3.6%).
- **Gross margin** differs by category (Groceries 16%, Electronics
  20%, Home & Kitchen 28%, Clothing 36%, Beauty & Care 34%). The
  23.5% margin target is the blended margin the mix yields —
  Electronics is 44% of revenue at 20% margin, so the old 26% plan
  was unreachable and has been replaced honestly, not hidden.
- **New vs repeat:** a customer's first order is "new", every later
  order "repeat". 392 customers bought in 2026; the repeat rate
  rises from 0% in January (a new shop has no repeat base) to 67%
  in December.
- **Returns rate** is volatile month to month (1.5–5.8%) because a
  single returned TV is PKR 42,000 — the pack shows the rate and
  the line count together so a spike is read correctly.

## Column notes

- `daily_sales.csv`: `line_amount_pkr` = qty × unit_price;
  `unit_cost_pkr` = unit_price × (1 − category margin), jittered
  ±2%; `returned` = 1 when the line was returned in full.
- `monthly_targets.csv`: `orders_target` = plan × 1.03 ÷ PKR 10,500
  (the expected net basket).
- `monthly_prior_year.csv`: 2025 revenue ≈ 2026 plan × 0.86 (±6%),
  so YoY growth reads +11% to +36% across 2026.
